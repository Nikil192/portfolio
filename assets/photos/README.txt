This folder is for your photography images.

To use your own photos on the photography page:
1. Save your image files in this folder (e.g., photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg).
2. Open photography.html in your code editor.
3. Replace the placeholder Unsplash URLs (e.g., src="https://images.unsplash.com/...") with your local paths: src="assets/photos/photo1.jpg"
4. Change the text in the <div class="photo-overlay"> to your own captions.
